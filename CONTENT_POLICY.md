# Content Policy

TLTP is a feedback forum, not a fact-verification service and not a channel that can issue official findings about any individual on behalf of the school.

## How AI handles content

- Relevant feedback that is harsh, vulgar or sensitive in wording is rewritten more respectfully; it is not rejected only because of its tone.
- AI must not add events, evidence or new allegations.
- AI must preserve the sender's main meaning and experience.
- Off-topic content receives `nothing`; the backend does not publish it and may delete it or place it in an internal queue.

## Do not use TLTP for

- Threats, calls for violence, self-harm or child abuse.
- Doxxing, phone numbers, addresses, accounts, student IDs or other private data.
- Spam, advertising, school-unrelated content or impersonating an official announcement.

## Reports

Published posts have a Report button. Moderators may hide, delete, restore or create a safety alert. GitHub Issues do not accept reports about real people or real posts.
