# Moderation Policy

TLTP does not use a queue to hold a post merely because the sender used harsh wording. Luna must rewrite the language respectfully when the feedback remains on topic.

AI responses have two branches:

- `publish` + `public_text`: publish immediately.
- `nothing` + `public_text: null`: do not publish; delete or place in an internal queue if classification is needed.

Moderators handle reports after publication. Severe safety risks create an internal alert; do not soften content until the risk is hidden.
