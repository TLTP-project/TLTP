# Privacy Notice

TLTP does not present itself as the school's official website. The public surface shows only processed content and an anonymous alias or role title.

The backend may store a Google account reference, role, raw feedback, IP address, user-agent, timestamps, AI result and audit logs for anti-spam controls, report handling and incident investigation. Private data is not readable by the public client; only authorized backend/admin operators may view the original, and access must be audited.

Users can remove a post from the public surface through soft delete. Retention periods, data-support requests and permanent deletion procedures must be finalized with the project owner before a public launch. This document is not legal advice.
